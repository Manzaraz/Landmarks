# Welcome to Landmarks!
I am excited to introduce this wonderful app designed for iPhone, Apple Watch, and Mac. 
With Landmarks, you can save all those dream locations you've always wanted to visit.
One of the great advantages of my application is its integration with the notification center. Thanks to this, you will receive reminders when you are near those places you have marked as favorites, so you won't miss the chance to explore and enjoy each one of them.
We hope that Landmarks helps you turn your travel dreams into reality!
